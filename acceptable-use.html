Bot-Hosting.net Acceptable Use Policy

This policy defines what is allowed and what is prohibited when using our Discord bot hosting services. By using Bot-Hosting.net, you agree to comply with the rules set out below.

Last updated: 10/05/2026


1. Scope and covered users

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to anyone who accesses or uses Bot-Hosting.net, including:

  • Visitors to our website
  • Account holders using our free or paid hosting plans
  • Sub-users granted access to a server by another account holder
  • Any third party connecting to or interacting with services hosted on our infrastructure

Our services consist of containerised hosting environments primarily designed for running Discord bots, along with related features such as databases, file storage, backups, and console access. Account holders are responsible for ensuring that any sub-user or third party they grant access to also complies with this policy.

2. Permitted use

Our services are intended for the lawful operation of Discord bots and similar lightweight applications. Permitted use includes, but is not limited to:

  • Hosting Discord bots that comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Developer Policy
  • Running automation, moderation, utility, music, or community-management bots
  • Operating small-scale web applications, APIs, or backend services compatible with our supported runtimes
  • Storing files and data reasonably required for the operation of your hosted application

3. Prohibited use

You may not use our services for any activity that is illegal, harmful, abusive, or that violates the rights of others. The following activities are strictly prohibited:

3.1 Illegal content and activities

  • Any activity unlawful under the laws of France, the European Union, or the country in which you reside
  • Storing, hosting, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content sexualising minors
  • Hosting content that promotes terrorism, violent extremism, or incites violence against individuals or groups
  • Distributing pirated software, copyrighted material, counterfeit goods, or content infringing intellectual property rights
  • Hosting or distributing malware, spyware, ransomware, viruses, or any malicious code
  • Facilitating human trafficking, illegal drug sales, illegal firearms sales, or any other criminal activity

3.2 Abuse of Discord and third-party platforms

  • Operating selfbots or any bot that violates Discord's Terms of Service or Developer Policy
  • Mass-messaging, spamming, or unsolicited advertising on Discord or any other platform
  • Raid bots, nuke bots, or any tool designed to disrupt Discord servers or harass their members
  • Token grabbers, account stealers, or any tool designed to compromise user credentials
  • Bypassing rate limits, anti-spam measures, or other protections of any third-party service

3.3 Attacks and harmful activity

  • Launching denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks against any system or network
  • Running brute-force, credential-stuffing, or password-guessing attacks
  • Performing unauthorised port scanning, vulnerability scanning, or reconnaissance against systems you do not own
  • Phishing, social engineering, or any attempt to deceive users into disclosing sensitive information
  • Hosting command-and-control (C2) infrastructure for botnets or other malicious networks

3.4 Resource abuse

  • Cryptocurrency mining or any form of distributed computing primarily designed to consume CPU, GPU, memory, or bandwidth
  • Excessive disk usage unrelated to the legitimate operation of a Discord bot or hosted application
  • Using our services as a proxy, VPN, or tunnel to disguise the origin of network traffic
  • Operating public file-sharing, streaming, or content-distribution services that disproportionately consume bandwidth or storage
  • Running multiple accounts to circumvent free-tier limits or any other restriction

3.5 Harmful and abusive content

  • Harassment, threats, doxing, or stalking of individuals
  • Hate speech or content discriminating against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, deepfakes, or content created without the subject's consent
  • Content promoting self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders

3.6 Fraud and payment abuse

  • Use of stolen, fraudulent, or unauthorised payment methods
  • Filing fraudulent chargebacks or payment disputes
  • Abusing referral, affiliate, or promotional programs through fake accounts or other deceptive means
  • Reselling or redistributing our services without prior written authorisation
